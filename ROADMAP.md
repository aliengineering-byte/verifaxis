# Roadmap

## v0.1 — deterministic research runtime

- Typed evidence, residuals, conservative controller, JSON traces.
- Safe arithmetic and restricted pure-function verification.
- Offline smoke baselines, metrics, reports, and verifier fault injection.
- OpenAI-compatible black-box adapter plus deterministic replay model.

## Future work

These items are proposals, not implemented claims:

- preregistered public math and isolated code benchmark manifests;
- multiple pinned black-box and open-weight model families;
- learned controllers and soft-prompt/state adapters around frozen weights;
- Z3 and formal-checker integrations;
- scientific simulators, CFD/PDE verification, and richer residuals;
- ResiliReplay-compatible fault schedules;
- verifier marketplace and policy/audit integrations.

Hosted services, billing, authentication, enterprise dashboards, and Kubernetes are intentionally out of scope.
