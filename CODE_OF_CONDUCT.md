# Code of Conduct

VerifAxis follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, and protect private information. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing others' private information.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban behavior they judge inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant ladder: correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban, proportionate to impact and pattern. This text is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1 under CC BY 4.0.
